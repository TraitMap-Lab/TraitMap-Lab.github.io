Biography

學術簡介

About

鄭舒允副教授任職於國立中興大學農藝學系，研究專長為基因體學與分子育種。取得博士學位後，赴康乃爾大學 Edward Buckler 實驗室從事玉米 miRNA eQTL GWAS 研究，後於中央研究院植物暨微生物學研究所應用 RNA-seq 與 BSA-seq 探討玉米減數分裂中的基因調控與甲基化機制。此後於經緯航太科技任職兩年，累積無人機（UAV）智慧農業實務經驗，再於成功大學從事蝴蝶蘭抗鐮孢菌基因鑑定研究，為蝴蝶蘭抗病 GWAS 奠立系統性基礎。

Dr. Shu-Yun Chen is an Associate Professor in the Department of Agronomy at National Chung Hsing University (NCHU). After completing her Ph.D., she conducted maize miRNA eQTL GWAS research in Edward Buckler's lab at Cornell University, followed by postdoctoral research at Academia Sinica applying RNA-seq and BSA-seq to study gene regulation and methylation during maize meiosis. She then worked at GeoSat Aerospace & Technology for two years, gaining practical expertise in UAV-based smart agriculture, before joining NCKU to investigate Fusarium resistance genes in Phalaenopsis orchids.

目前於中興大學持續推動絲瓜、甘藷、苦瓜等作物之抗病研究，結合單株 GWAS（spGWAS）與 RNA-seq 開發分子標記，加速抗病品種育成。

At NCHU, her lab continues advancing disease-resistance research in luffa, sweet potato, and bitter melon, combining single-plant GWAS (spGWAS) with RNA-seq to develop molecular markers that accelerate resistant variety breeding.
