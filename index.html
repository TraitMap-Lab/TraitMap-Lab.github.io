從作物性狀到分子標記，推進精準育種

Mapping crop traits into precision breeding tools

TraitMap Lab 連結田間性狀、基因體資料與分子育種，利用 GWAS、RNA-seq、BSA-seq 與高通量表型分析，解析作物抗病與逆境反應，並開發可應用於育種的分子標記。

TraitMap Lab connects field traits, genomic data, and molecular breeding. We integrate GWAS, RNA-seq, BSA-seq, and high-throughput phenotyping to study crop disease resistance and stress responses, then translate discoveries into breeding-ready molecular markers.

Lab Signature

TraitMap Lab 的研究特色

What makes TraitMap Lab distinctive

我們不只做單一技術，而是把「性狀觀察、基因體分析、候選基因驗證、育種應用」串成完整研究路徑。

Our strength is not a single technique. We connect trait observation, genomic analysis, candidate-gene validation, and breeding application into one research path.

01

從田間問題出發

Trait-first questions

以抗病、逆境、產量與重要農藝性狀為起點，讓研究題目貼近作物改良需求。

We begin with disease resistance, stress response, yield, and agronomic traits that matter for crop improvement.

02

多層級資料整合

Multi-layer data integration

整合表型、基因型、轉錄體與族群資料，解析性狀背後的遺傳調控。

We integrate phenotypes, genotypes, transcriptomes, and population data to understand trait regulation.

03

走向育種應用

Toward breeding tools

將研究成果轉化為分子標記與篩選策略，支援抗病品種與精準育種。

We translate discoveries into molecular markers and screening strategies for precision breeding.

Research

我們如何把性狀變成育種線索

How we turn traits into breeding leads

實驗室訓練學生同時理解作物、分子實驗與資料分析，從研究設計到結果詮釋都能看見完整脈絡。

Students learn to connect crop biology, molecular experiments, and data analysis, seeing the full logic from study design to biological interpretation.

Step 1

表型與材料

Phenotypes & materials

建立作物材料、病害或逆境處理，以及可量化的性狀資料。

Build plant materials, disease or stress assays, and measurable trait data.

Step 2

基因體定位

Genomic mapping

利用 GWAS、spGWAS、BSA-seq 或族群資料找出關聯區域。

Use GWAS, spGWAS, BSA-seq, or population datasets to identify associated loci.

Step 3

轉錄體與候選基因

Transcriptomes & genes

透過 RNA-seq 與表現分析縮小候選基因並推論調控機制。

Use RNA-seq and expression analysis to refine candidate genes and mechanisms.

Step 4

分子標記與應用

Markers & application

開發分子標記，協助抗病、耐逆境與重要性狀的育種選拔。

Develop markers to support selection for resistance, stress tolerance, and key traits.

Join Us

適合想把生物問題和資料分析接起來的學生

For students who want to connect biology and data

PI & Background

主持人與研究背景

Principal investigator and background

鄭舒允副教授任職於國立中興大學農藝學系，研究專長為基因體學與分子育種。取得博士學位後，赴康乃爾大學 Edward Buckler 實驗室從事玉米 miRNA eQTL GWAS 研究，後於中央研究院植物暨微生物學研究所應用 RNA-seq 與 BSA-seq 探討玉米減數分裂中的基因調控與甲基化機制。

Dr. Shu-Yun Chen is an Associate Professor in the Department of Agronomy at National Chung Hsing University. After completing his Ph.D., he conducted maize miRNA eQTL GWAS research in Edward Buckler's lab at Cornell University, followed by postdoctoral research at Academia Sinica applying RNA-seq and BSA-seq to study gene regulation and methylation during maize meiosis.

他也曾於經緯航太科技累積 UAV 智慧農業實務經驗，並於成功大學從事蝴蝶蘭抗鐮孢菌基因鑑定研究。現在於中興大學推動絲瓜、甘藷、苦瓜等作物之抗病與重要性狀研究。

He also gained UAV-based smart agriculture experience at GeoSat Aerospace & Technology and studied Fusarium resistance genes in Phalaenopsis orchids at NCKU. At NCHU, his lab advances disease-resistance and trait research in luffa, sweet potato, bitter melon, and related crops.
