恭賀大學部學生趙芝瑩、盧昱婷研究提案獲得肯定

Congratulations to Chih-Ying Chao and Yu-Ting Lu (Ashli)

恭喜兩位同學研究提案獲得國科會大專學生研究計畫肯定，展現優秀研究潛力。兩項主題分別延伸實驗室在作物抗病、逆境反應與分子育種上的核心方向。

Two undergraduate researchers received recognition from the NSTC Undergraduate Student Research Project program. Their projects extend the lab's core interests in crop disease resistance, stress responses, and molecular breeding.
