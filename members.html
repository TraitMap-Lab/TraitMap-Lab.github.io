Principal Investigator

鄭舒允

Shu-Yun Chen

Shu-Yun Chen 鄭舒允
副教授 · 農藝學系 Associate Professor · Dept. of Agronomy

專研功能性基因體學與精準育種，整合 GWAS、RNA-seq、BSA-seq 解析農園藝作物抗病機制，致力開發分子標記加速育種流程。

Research focuses on functional genomics and precision breeding, integrating GWAS, RNA-seq, and BSA-seq to uncover disease-resistance mechanisms and develop molecular markers for accelerated breeding in agricultural and horticultural crops.
